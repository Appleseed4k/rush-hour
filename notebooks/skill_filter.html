How bayes4's skill filter works

One unhelped simulated student (agency 0), as bayes4 sees them. Step through a puzzle move by move, then see how the finished puzzle moves the belief about skill. Distances of 11 or more share a bin.

1. Before the puzzleA normal belief over skill per distance, one per τ. The plan uses the best τ.
2. During the puzzleEach move of the student's own updates the belief about this puzzle's difficulty z.
3. After the puzzleA Laplace step folds the puzzle's moves into the mean and covariance. Nothing else is stored.
4. Reweight τEach τ's filter gains the evidence for its update. Old evidence fades by 0.99 a puzzle.
